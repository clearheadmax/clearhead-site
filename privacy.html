Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Clearhead ("we", "us", "the app") is a social app that helps people who are sober, sober-curious, or just exploring find each other, discover alcohol-free-friendly venues, and share moments from nights out without the drinks. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how it's protected, and the rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

Clearhead is operated by Diserve Ventures Oy, registered in Espoo, Finland, business ID (Y-tunnus) 2378021-4. For the purposes of GDPR, we are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. On the App Store, Clearhead is published under the developer account of Maximilian Opas, who runs the app on behalf of Diserve Ventures Oy.

Contact for privacy questions: max@diserve.com

2. What data we collect

We collect only what the app actually needs to function. Specifically:

CategoryWhat's included
Account data Email address, password (stored as a secure hash — we never see or store your actual password), account creation date.
Profile data Name, age, profile photo, bio, sober-since date (optional), interests, self-identification (sober / sober-curious / exploring), and whether your Moments are public or connections-only.
Location data Your device's location, used to show nearby people and venues. For people and Moments it is rounded to an approximate location (about 1 km) before it leaves your device: we store only this approximate location on your profile and on Moments you post, and a Moment may also show a general area label like "Helsinki, Finland" if you don't tag a specific venue. Other users can see roughly how far away you are, never your exact location. To find venues right around you, the app sends a more precise location (rounded to about 100 m) to Google Places only; we never store or share that one.
Photos Your profile photo and photos you post as "Moments" (a paired photo of where you are and a selfie), uploaded to our storage provider.
Social/relationship data Connections between you and other users, events you host or mark interest in, drinks you favorite, venues you save, community tags you or others apply to venues, comments you write on Moments, Moments you repost, and friends you tag in your Moments (only your accepted connections can be tagged, and anyone can remove a tag of themselves).
Messages Direct messages you send to and receive from your accepted connections (text and time). Only you and the person you're talking to can read them. You can unsend your own messages. If someone reports a conversation, we may review the reported messages for moderation.
Drink-less plan and check-ins Only if you choose to make a plan: your goal (for example drink less or Dry January), roughly how many drinks a week you had before, your weekly target, the price of a drink, the reasons you pick, your check-in reminder time, and your daily check-ins (how many drinks you had on a day). This is private to you: it is never shown to other users, not even your connections.
Purchases If you subscribe to Clearhead Pro: which plan you have and when it renews or expires. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.
Content Captions, tags, and comments-equivalent content you post.
Safety data Accounts you block, and reports you file about other users' content — used only for moderation.
Device/technical data Basic device information needed to run the app and deliver local notifications (we do not currently use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK — see Section 5).

3. Why we collect it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the app's core functionality (contractual necessity) — your profile, location, and content are the app; without them Discover, Venues, Moments, and Connections don't work.
  • To keep the community safe (legitimate interest) — blocking and reporting data is used solely for moderation.
  • Your drink-less plan and check-ins (your explicit consent) — this information can say something about your health, so we only collect it when you choose to make a plan, we use it only to show you your own progress, and you can delete it at any time (Profile → Settings → Delete account, or by contacting us).
  • Where you've given consent — e.g. granting location permission, or opting into specific notification types, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings or your device's permission settings.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

4. How your data is stored and secured

  • Data is stored with Supabase, a managed Postgres database provider. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access to your data within our database is enforced with row-level security rules — for example, your private Moments are only readable by you and your accepted connections, never by other users or, in ordinary operation, by us directly querying around those rules.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing.
  • Our database is hosted by Supabase in the EU (Ireland). Some of the providers listed in Section 5 (for example Google and Apple) may process data outside the EU/EEA; where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We work with a small number of service providers ("processors") who handle data strictly on our behalf:

We do not currently use any advertising network, analytics SDK, or marketing/tracking tool (no Google Analytics, Meta/Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, etc. are integrated into the app as of this writing).

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app. This removes your profile, Moments, connections, favorites, photos, messages, and drink-less plan and check-ins immediately, except where we're required to retain something longer for legal reasons (e.g. active safety reports under investigation).

7. Your rights under GDPR

As an EU/EEA resident (or resident of a jurisdiction with similar protections), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data (most of this you can already edit directly in your Profile)
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in Finland, this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at max@diserve.com.

8. Children's privacy

Clearhead is not intended for anyone under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll notify you in the app before the changes take effect. Continued use of the app after that point means you accept the updated policy.
