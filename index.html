Clearhead

A social club for people going out without the alcohol.

Clearhead is for anyone choosing a night out without alcohol — sober, sober-curious, or just taking a break. No pressure, no judgment, no interrogation about why. Connect with others nearby doing the same thing, discover real alcohol-free-friendly bars, restaurants, and cafes, and keep track of the mocktails and NA drinks worth going back for.

Find your people Meet sober-curious folks nearby who are down to actually go out.
Discover new spots NA-friendly bars, restaurants, and cafes worth the trip — sourced from real places, plus tags from the community on which ones are actually alcohol-free-friendly.
Drink something great Save your favorite mocktails, NA beers, wines, and functional drinks.
