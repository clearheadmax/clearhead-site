Events

Plans, minus the hangover.

Join meetups near you or host your own: sober raves, run clubs, game nights, mocktail classes. Tap "I'm in" and you're on the list.

What we're about

Nobody here is going to ask you why.

No pressure

Sober for years, cutting back, or just driving tonight. Everyone belongs, whatever the reason.

No labels

Call it sober, sober-curious, or nothing at all. Your profile says as much or as little as you want.

No interrogation

Questioning or minimizing anyone's reasons isn't allowed here. It's in our community guidelines.

How it works

Three steps to a better night.

Make your profile

Add a photo, a few interests, and how you'd describe yourself. Only if you want to.

Pick a spot

Find an NA-friendly bar, cafe or restaurant nearby, or host a meetup of your own.

Share the moment

Snap where you are and what you're drinking. Your circle sees the night, not the hangover.

Safety first

Built to keep it a good place to be.

Approximate location

Your location is rounded to about 1 km before it leaves your phone. Nobody sees exactly where you are.

Block anyone

Blocking instantly hides someone's content and stops any further contact.

Report in one tap

Every Moment, profile and event can be reported. We review every report within 24 hours.

18+ and private

Adults only. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
